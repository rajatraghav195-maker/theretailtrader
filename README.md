# The Retail Trader

**Website: <https://theretailtrader.org>**

The Retail Trader is an educational platform for Indian stock markets. It brings NSE and BSE charting, stock screening, market data analysis and plain-language learning articles together in one place.

**Charts:** advanced charts for NSE and BSE stocks and indices at <https://theretailtrader.org/charting>

**Learn:** Market Intelligence articles and a learning game on the website.

**Pricing:** <https://theretailtrader.org/pricing>

**Contact:** support@theretailtrader.org

*The Retail Trader is an educational platform and is not registered with SEBI in any capacity. Nothing on the site is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.*
